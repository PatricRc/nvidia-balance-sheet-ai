READING GUIDE

From statement to evidence

  1. Choose a fiscal period. The statements identify whether each comparison is a reporting-date balance, quarter or year-to-date flow.
  2. Read the chapter conclusion and check the amounts and units underneath.
  3. Use Financial Statements to switch between the balance sheet, profit, cash flow, debt and liquidity. Open Notes & Evidence to trace a claim.

Balance sheets report amounts as of a date. Income and cash flow report activity over a period.
