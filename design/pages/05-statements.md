# 05 · Financial Statements

The statements chapter is a paper ledger with a switcher for **Balance sheet**, **Profit & loss**, **Cash flow**, **Debt**, and **Liquidity**. The switcher preserves the fiscal selection. Reset restores the interim preset and balance sheet.

| View | Presentation | Interim comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Balance sheet | Classified current and noncurrent assets and liabilities, then equity. Indented lines, subtotals, total assets and total liabilities plus equity. | July 26, 2026 vs January 25, 2026, as filed. |
| Profit & loss | Margin ladder followed by revenue, cost, operating expenses, other income, taxes and net income. Diluted EPS uses USD per share. | Three months ended July 26, 2026 vs July 27, 2025. |
| Cash flow | Opening-to-closing bridge with operating, investing and financing flows. Condensed activity totals with the source link for detailed lines. | Six months ended July 26, 2026 vs July 27, 2025. This is fiscal YTD. |
| Debt | Supplemental carrying amount schedule with short-term and long-term debt, noncurrent operating leases, and both net cash definitions. | Same dates as the balance sheet. |
| Liquidity | Supplemental current asset composition and obligations with working capital and coverage multiples. | Same dates as the balance sheet. |

Annual views compare FY2026 with FY2025. Accounting tables present USD millions with aligned digits, negative amounts in parentheses, zero dashes, and double rules beneath key totals. Supplemental schedules are derived from filing lines. The prototype gives a two-column view and links to the full filings, which supply further years, detailed cash flow lines, policies, and notes.
