# 03 · Debt & Net Cash

**Browser prototype.** The browser composition uses a split chapter opening, a zero-centered net cash comparison and a short debt classification strip. The layout table below is a future native report reference.

**Question.** How much interest-bearing debt is on the statement and how does the cash definition change the answer?

**Opening line.** Two readings of net cash. Lead with the cash-only position and show the broader position beside it. The server-rack image introduces the chapter and stays separate from the quantitative evidence.

| Zone | Native layout target and binding |
| --- | --- |
| Summary | y=120–232. Debt carrying amount, cash-only net cash, securities-inclusive net position. Use explicit negative signs and units. |
| Debt classification | x=224–828, y=248–610. Compare short-term and long-term carrying amounts on the same scale. Use labels and a table. |
| Capital ledger | x=844–1248. Include noncurrent operating leases in a separate row. The full filing supplies future commitments and maturities. |
| Definition and next action | Cash-only = cash and equivalents less debt. Broader = cash and equivalents plus current marketable securities less debt. Both point to the filing. |

**DAX.** The existing Long-Term Debt measure gives the noncurrent piece only. A complete debt total needs a reconciled current piece and an explicit reported zero rule. The broader net measure needs a reconciled combined securities value with mutually exclusive source mappings. For an optional leverage row, label existing Debt to Equity as noncurrent debt/equity until a complete debt numerator is available. Keep lease liabilities and off-balance sheet commitments separate.

**Check.** Q2 FY2027 $1.000B short-term plus $32.366B long-term equals $33.366B. Net cash is −$10.923B cash-only and $66.003B with reported current marketable securities. The $76.926B difference is the securities input. Use carrying amounts consistently and identify any restricted securities context from the filing.
